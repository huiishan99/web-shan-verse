<p>
I remember that in the past, my teacher would say that people like me like talking in dreams. But I only like talking in dreams; I am not living inside a dream.<br />
It feels very depressing. Other than my teacher, it seems like no one is really willing to understand what I am thinking.
</p>

<p>
Or rather, even if someone wants to understand me, aside from my teacher, most people simply do not have the ability to understand me.
</p>
