<p>
A few days ago, I told my boss that I like studying philosophy. I said existentialism has had a big influence on my life...
</p>

<p>
Then my boss said he also really likes Sartre.
</p>

<p>
But I actually do not like Sartre. The one I like is Camus. Sartre feels too violent to me. I am more like a reclusive druid who maintains balance, quietly loving this world (even though I do not look like one).
</p>

<p>
Back when my friend and I had an ideological rupture, it was like Camus and Sartre breaking apart. This story would not feel new even a thousand years from now.
</p>
