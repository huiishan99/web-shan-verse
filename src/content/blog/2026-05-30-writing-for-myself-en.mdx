<p>
I like writing little essays<br />
Actually, I also like writing poems in my own style
</p>

<p>
At first, I only wrote them for myself<br />
In the very early days, nobody liked my posts<br />
I did not care how other people saw me
</p>

<p>
I was just expressing my anger to the world<br />
Expressing my sadness<br />
Expressing my happiness
</p>

<p>As I kept writing... some people started to notice, and started to tell me they understood me</p>

<p>
Then I began to care<br />
Care about how they saw my writing
</p>

<p>
Gradually, it felt like I was no longer writing only for myself<br />
If I were only writing for myself<br />
I would not need to worry about format<br />
I would not need to worry about awkward sentences<br />
I would not need to worry about whether other people could understand<br />
I would not need other people's approval
</p>

<p>
Because there are more people I care about now<br />
And there are more connections tied to interests too
</p>

<p>But why bother?</p>

<p>
If I want to say something, then I should just say it<br />
Back then, it was exactly because I was honest about my weakness and my anger<br />
That I attracted the people who were truly worth staying connected with
</p>

<p>The people who care about me will not stop reading my little essays just because I go a bit crazy for a moment, right? haha</p>
