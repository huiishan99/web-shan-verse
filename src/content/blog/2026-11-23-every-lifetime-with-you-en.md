Today my partner and I went to order a Christmas cake, getting ready to spend Christmas with my good friend Mr. J, who is also from the same cohort as me, and his fiancée, who is preparing to come to Japan. While we were out, we also bought lots and lots of things we need to keep stocked at home, snacks, fruit, vegetables, and so on, just like what we usually do on weekends.

At checkout, I suddenly felt a little dazed. As I watched my partner scan all the items and pay for them, I suddenly had this feeling: this life seems like something I wished for very, very strongly a long, long time ago, as if from a previous life. It was as if I had lived through a time of great social turmoil and made this wish for such a stable life, so now I somehow feel as though a dream has come true. Although we are still worrying about whether we can earn enough money to retire soon, worrying about social changes, worrying about economic problems, worrying about tax policies, and so on, our life is very ordinary, but somehow, strangely, this everyday life with my partner makes me feel so at ease.

Although I do not follow any religion, I think perhaps God exists, or some highest rule controls my fate or something. I have always felt that meeting my partner was something very magical, as if we had summoned each other. The encounter was just right, our personalities complemented each other just right, and we were both honest and loving people, just right. I deliberately arrived late back then and still managed to meet my partner. The place I first rented was three minutes away from his, and he had even visited the university where I did my bachelor's degree before! My hometown is in northeastern Malaysia, he is from northeastern China, and we met in northeastern Japan. Even his birthday on the lunar calendar and mine on the solar calendar happened to overlap because of the time difference! All, all of these coincidences were making it our destiny to meet, and even to fall in love.

So I have always felt that he is a real little angel. He always stays by my side and never gives up on me. He always respects my decisions, respects my dreams, and secretly buys me a whole pile of things I have said I wanted (but very often I am just casually saying I want something, I do not actually want it that much 😂). Although we sometimes argue and misunderstand each other, in the end we always clear up the misunderstandings and turn our hearts toward each other. I cannot help telling him I love him every day, on my own initiative. Every time he first sees me after work, he always smiles so happily, as if no matter how much pressure he is under at work, it is all worth it. Of course, it actually hurts me to see how much pressure he is under at work. I wish so much that I could go to work with him, but sometimes not every wish can come true so easily.

Although life keeps moving forward dynamically and there are always many new problems, my partner often feels helpless with my own challenges, and I often feel helpless when he is dealing with his own challenges. But I think the greatest love and courage is that no matter how difficult things get, we will keep moving toward each other. I love my good friend, husband, most sincere partner, my faithful family member, my soulmate so much. If people have another life, I hope that in every lifetime my love and I can be this close…
